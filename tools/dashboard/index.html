How the energy and cost are estimated

A background logger records, once a minute, the CPU time used by the bot's own copy of Geometry Dash and by its Python training and test processes. Each CPU-second counts as 1.75 joules: the laptop processor's default 28 W package power spread over its 16 threads. The screen, graphics and the rest of the laptop are not counted, so the real figure is somewhat higher. Cost uses the U.S. Energy Information Administration's latest California residential average price. Tracking began on 18 November 2026; earlier test runs were not measured. The numbers refresh every evening around 8 pm.
