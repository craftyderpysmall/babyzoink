    How ratings work

    A rating is the modeled difficulty at which a typical entered section has a 50% chance of being survived for 10 seconds. The model uses time exposed and deaths. Sections the bot never reaches provide no evidence. Later sections may be entered by a stronger subset of attempts.

    Drills and one- or two-star levels anchor zero. Other rated non-demons fall below tier 1. Demon anchors use the GD Demon Ladder. Ratings appear when observations cover the estimated rating and support calibration. Intervals reflect model uncertainty conditional on the recorded sections.

    Attempts to section clear average confirmed clears across runs and sections. The observed entries and deaths include failed attempts.
